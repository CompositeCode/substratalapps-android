## Using this screen

Enter the email and password for your Substratal account and tap **Sign in**. The eye button shows or hides the password. Your password manager can fill both fields.

If your account has two-factor authentication, you'll be asked for a code next. See [Two-factor challenge](auth-02.md).

Error messages never say whether an account exists. *That email and password don't match* covers both cases. After 5 failed attempts in 15 minutes, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes. More in [Sign in](../../getting-started/sign-in.md).
