## Using this screen

- **Search:** type at least 3 characters of a name or email, or a full `usr_` ID.
- **Filter:** tap **Active**, **Invited** or **Suspended**. Tap it again to clear it.
- **Open someone:** tap their row.
- **Invite user** is shown if you have `users.manage`.

Pull down to refresh; scroll to load more. The newest accounts are listed first. See [Users](../../guides/users.md).
