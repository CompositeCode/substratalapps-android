## Using this screen

The top card shows your email, your account ID (`usr_…`) and your status. Below are:

- **Security:** two-factor and sessions
- **Devices & notifications**
- **Privacy:** export your data, or delete your account
- **Plan & Billing**
- **Settings:** appearance
- **Help & documentation:** this site
- **Sign out:** asks you to confirm, then ends your session and removes it from this device

The footer shows the app version and the API version it's using. Include both when you report a problem.
