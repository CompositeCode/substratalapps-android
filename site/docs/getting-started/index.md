# Getting started

1. [Install the app](install.md) on your phone, tablet or Chromebook.
2. [Sign in](sign-in.md) with your Substratal account and your two-factor code.
3. [Find your way around](navigation.md): what's in the bottom bar, the Manage hub and the side menu on bigger screens.

!!! tip "What you'll see depends on your role"
    The app shows only what your account is allowed to use. A support agent sees Users and Access, a developer sees their own Applications and keys, and a superadmin sees everything, including Operations. See [Permissions and roles](../reference/permissions.md).
